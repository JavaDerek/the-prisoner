A referee can rule on these kinds of effect and no others:
wear (lower a property: scraping, chipping, bending, filing, striking, damaging or dulling a thing),
restore (raise or reset a property),
reveal (learn a property's true value: examine, inspect, check, look under or into),
conceal (hide a thing, raise concealment),
expose (uncover a hidden thing, lower concealment),
noise (a perceptible event with no state change),
open (make a way out passable in one act),
close (shut a way out),
leave (go out through a way out),
derive (make a new thing from part of something and keep it).

Every one of these kinds changes the state of a single, bounded object or fixture -- an item, a lock, a tile, a door.
